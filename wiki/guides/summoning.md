# Summoning and pity

Scrolls recruit new ninja on the **Summon** screen. You start with {{num:economy.start.scrolls}} scrolls, and every first clear in the story pays more.

## Costs

- One summon costs {{num:economy.pullCost.single}} scrolls.
- A 10× summon costs {{num:economy.pullCost.ten}} scrolls and always includes at least one {{tier:gacha.tenPullGuaranteeTier}} or better.

| Tier | Rarity | Chance per summon |

Tiers follow canon power, but every tier stays useful: stars and levels matter more than rarity.

## The Kage guarantee

If you go {{num:gacha.pity}} summons without a Kage, the next one is guaranteed to be a {{tier:gacha.pityTier}}. The counter:

- is shown on the Summon screen;
- carries across every banner;
- starts again whenever you summon a Kage.

## Banners

- The **Standard Summon** is always open and holds every ninja who has joined the pools.
- Every story arc has its own **banner**, which opens when you reach the arc. Its featured ninja share {{pct:gacha.rateUpShare}} of their tier's chance.
- Villains join the pools only after you clear their arc. Until then, banners list them as "joins after …".
- The exact rates of every banner are on [Banners and rates](wiki:banners).

## Duplicates and stars

- Summoning a ninja you already own adds a **star**: {{plus:stats.starBonus}} to all their stats, up to {{num:stats.starCap}}★.
- Past {{num:stats.starCap}}★, a duplicate turns into Ryo instead.

## Alternate forms

Late-game power comes from alternate forms, such as [Naruto Uzumaki (Sage Mode)](wiki:character/naruto_sage). Forms are separate ninja in the pools: they level separately, and a team can only field one form of each ninja.
