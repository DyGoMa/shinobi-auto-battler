# The Nature Wheel and counters

Most ninja and enemies fight with a chakra **nature**. Natures beat each other in a circle:

**{{cycle:natureWheel.cycle}}**

Each nature beats the next one. The [interactive chart](wiki:nature-wheel) shows every matchup.

## What "beats" means

- An **effective** hit, where your nature beats the target's, deals {{x:natureWheel.advantage}} damage. The number pops in the nature's colour with **EFFECTIVE!**
- A **resisted** hit, where the target's nature beats yours, deals {{x:natureWheel.disadvantage}}.
- It works both ways: a ninja whose nature beats the boss hits it harder **and** takes less damage from it.

## Ninja with several natures

They **attack** with whichever of their natures is best against each target, and **defend** with their first nature. [Kakashi Hatake](wiki:character/kakashi) (Lightning, Earth, Water) hits a Water enemy with Earth Style, but Wind attacks still hit him hard, because he defends with Lightning.

## Special cases

- **Taijutsu specialists** such as [Rock Lee](wiki:character/lee) and [Might Guy](wiki:character/guy) have no nature. They are never resisted, and they ignore {{pct:combat.taijutsuDefIgnore}} of enemy DEF.
- Ninja and enemies with **no nature** are neutral against everything.
- Some bosses **switch nature** mid-fight, and some boss specials use a different nature from the boss itself. The Story map lists every nature you'll face in a battle.

## Bringing counters

1. On the Story map, check the battle's **Enemy natures**.
2. Find the nature that beats them on the wheel.
3. On the Team screen, ninja marked ▲ counter this battle's enemies, and the matchup stars rate the whole team.
4. A counter from your bench catches up fast: ninja {{num:economy.catchUp.gap}} or more levels behind your best one level up {{pct:economy.catchUp.discount}} cheaper.

> You don't need a perfect counter for every battle. A neutral team wins most fights; counters make the hard ones easier, and they decide [Jutsu Clashes](wiki:guide/jutsu-clash) too.
