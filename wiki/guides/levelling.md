# Levelling and economy

Two currencies run the game: **📜 scrolls** summon ninja, and **🪙 Ryo** levels them up.

## Levelling up

- Level ninja on the **Roster** screen with +1, +5 or Max.
- A level costs {{num:economy.levelUpCost.base}} Ryo, plus {{num:economy.levelUpCost.growth}} for every level the ninja already has. The highest level is {{num:stats.levelCap}}.
- Every level adds {{pct:stats.levelMult.growth}} of a ninja's starting stats.
- **Catch-up discount:** a ninja {{num:economy.catchUp.gap}} or more levels behind your highest-level ninja levels up {{pct:economy.catchUp.discount}} cheaper, so bringing a nature counter off the bench is cheap.

## Earning scrolls and Ryo

| Source | Pays |
|---|---|
| First clear of a battle | Scrolls and Ryo; boss battles pay {{x:economy.bossNodeBonusMult}} |
| Replaying a battle | Ryo and a few scrolls |
| Clearing a whole arc | A one-time bonus of scrolls and Ryo |
| Boss Rush rounds | Scrolls and Ryo for every round cleared, every run |
| The tutorial | {{num:tutorial.rewards.scrolls}} scrolls and {{num:tutorial.rewards.ryo}} Ryo, once |
| Duplicates past {{num:stats.starCap}}★ | Ryo |

Rewards grow as the story goes on, so later battles pay more.

## Spending well

- Level your **main team** first, then one or two counters for the natures that give you trouble.
- Don't spread Ryo across everyone you own: four strong ninja beat eight weak ones.
- Stuck on a boss? Replay the previous battle a few times for Ryo, then level up.
- Save scrolls for 10× summons when you can: each one guarantees a {{tier:gacha.tenPullGuaranteeTier}} or better.
