# Team composition

A team is **three members and a ★ Leader**. The Leader fights too, and gives the team a passive buff on top.

## Roles and the lane

Battles happen on a single lane. Your ninja line up by range, front to back, and can't walk through each other.

| Role | Where it fights | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| 🛡️ **Tank** | Front line, in contact | Holds enemies and soaks hits. Its Ultimate **taunts**: enemies must attack it while it takes less damage. |
| ⚔️ **Striker** | Front, or right behind a Tank | Heavy damage. Its Ultimate hits one enemy hard, or everything near its target. |
| 🎯 **Ranged** | Back row | Steady damage from behind the front line. |
| ✚ **Support** | Back row | **Heals** the whole team or **raises its ATK** with its Ultimate. |

Only the front fighter and one Striker right behind it can reach an enemy in melee. A third melee ninja mostly waits in line, and the Team screen warns you when that happens. Ultimates are jutsu, so they reach any enemy.

## The Leader slot

Every ninja has their own Leader buff, shown on the Team screen and on their Wiki page. A buff helps either the whole team or a group: a squad like Team 7, a village, a nature, a role or a tier.

- A buff for a group is {{x:leader.scopedMult}} as strong as a whole-team buff, so match your Leader to your team.
- A Kage-tier Leader's buff is {{x:leader.tierMult.kage}} as strong.
- Some battles fix the Leader, or have no Leader at all (the Survival Test is Team 7 without their sensei).

## Forced and benched ninja

Some battles follow the story. They can:

- **force** ninja into your team: you get them as guests at the battle's level if you don't own them;
- **fix the Leader**, as when you fight as the Third Hokage;
- **bench** ninja, who sit that battle out.

Forced ninja always play and take their slots first.

## Picking a team for a battle

1. Open the battle on the Story map and read the **enemy natures**.
2. On the Team screen, the **matchup stars** rate your team against those natures. ▲ marks ninja who counter them, ▼ marks ninja they counter.
3. **✨ Auto** picks a team by power and nature matchup. It's a good start: then swap in your favourites.

## Sample teams for common bosses

> These are examples. Any ninja with the same role and a nature that counters the boss will do, so check the boss's natures on the [Enemies and bosses](wiki:enemies) pages first.

**A boss with big ⚠ specials** (most arc bosses)

- A **Tank** to guard: a Tank that clashes pulls the jutsu onto itself.
- A **Striker whose nature beats the boss**, to Overpower its specials.
- A **Support** to heal through whatever gets past.
- Against a Water boss, for example: [Gaara](wiki:character/gaara), [Kakashi Hatake](wiki:character/kakashi) (Earth beats Water), [Ino Yamanaka](wiki:character/ino) and [Sakura Haruno](wiki:character/sakura).

**A boss that summons reinforcements**

- Area Ultimates clear the extra enemies: [Temari](wiki:character/temari), [Shino Aburame](wiki:character/shino) or [Haku](wiki:character/haku).

**A boss behind a shield**

- Break it with big single-target Ultimates, like [Naruto Uzumaki](wiki:character/naruto)'s Rasengan or [Sasuke Uchiha](wiki:character/sasuke)'s Chidori.

**A boss that reflects damage**

- [Neji Hyuga](wiki:enemy/e_neji_boss)'s Eight Trigrams: Palm Rotation throws damage back for a few seconds. Bring a healer and fire Ultimates after the stance ends.

**A boss that heals or comes back**

- Regeneration, lifesteal and revives are beaten by steady damage and stuns, such as [Shikamaru Nara](wiki:character/shikamaru)'s Shadow Possession Jutsu.

**Protect missions with divers**

- A Tank can't block a diver. Ranged ninja turn and shoot it before it reaches the escort.
