# Achievements

Achievements are goals across the whole game. They **unlock by themselves** as you play, including for things you did before they existed, and you **claim their rewards** on the Achievements screen: tap 🏆 at the top of the screen.

## Rewards

- **🪙 Ryo** for levelling up.
- **🎟️ Summon tickets:** each one is a free summon on any open banner.
- **🎫 Rare+ summon tickets:** a summon that is guaranteed to be {{tier:achievements.rareTicketMinTier}} or better. The big milestones give these.
- **🌟 An exclusive ninja:** completing Part I and Part II earns [Naruto Uzumaki (Nine-Tails Chakra Mode)](wiki:character/naruto_chakramode). This form never appears in any banner, and it joins at the level of your best-levelled Naruto.

When a reward is waiting, 🏆 and the Home tab show a red dot. **🎁 Claim all** takes every waiting reward at once.

Use tickets on the **Summon** screen: the ticket buttons appear under the normal summon buttons. Ticket summons count toward the Kage guarantee like any other summon.

## The four kinds

- **Story:** finish the tutorial, clear each part, and take on Hard mode.
- **Collection:** recruit more ninja, cover every nature, collect every form of one ninja, and max out a ninja's level.
- **Combat:** win without losing anyone, win with a Poor or Bad nature matchup, overpower enemy jutsu in Jutsu Clashes, beat a boss while under-levelled, and survive the Boss Rush.
- **Account:** your first summon, linking a Google account, playing on different days, and clearing Daily challenges.

Every achievement's exact goal and reward is on the [Achievements](wiki:achievements) pages.

## Tips

- **Giant Killer** needs your team's average level to be at least {{num:achievements.underdogLevels}} below the boss's. A team that counters the boss's nature makes it much easier.
- **Against the Odds** counts the nature matchup on the Team screen when the battle starts. Pick a team that says Poor or Bad and win anyway.
- **Clash Master** counts Overpowers only: fire an Ultimate into a ⚠ wind-up with a ninja whose nature beats the jutsu. See [Jutsu Clash explained](wiki:guide/jutsu-clash).
- Combat achievements count from the moment they arrived in the game; everything else checks your whole save.
