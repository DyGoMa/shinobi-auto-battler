7 · Portrait spec, test slots and prompts

Generate a test portrait with one of the prompts, then load it here (it stays in this browser only). Every slot on this page switches to your image at once, so you can judge the frame, size and treatment in every context. A flat background (the magenta the prompts ask for, or the white Gemini sometimes gives instead) is keyed out on load, as the ingest tool will do; Flip mirrors a portrait that came out facing the wrong way.

Slots and sizes

One 256×256 image serves every context: token (56 · 40 px), the ult bar (44 px), the dialogue frame (96 px), the cut-in (80 px), the boss card (120 px), the Kage reveal (full). Busts are framed chin to mid-chest with the eyes on the upper third, so the circle crop keeps the face.

8 · Palette, type, effects and icons

  

    
Part I palette
Warm daylight. Paper and wood chrome, the headband plate for the tab bar, orange as the one hot accent.

    
Shippuden palette
Cooler and higher contrast. Black cloth and dark steel chrome, crimson as the accent, a red cloud only on Akatsuki surfaces.

    
Nature colours (unchanged)
The five nature colours stay as they are in the game today; they are already the tint of every chip, pip and floating number.

    
Type
Jutsu Clash — Overpower!
霧隠れの鬼人 · 口寄せの術
Body stays the system font: Team power 3,240 · Recommended 3,100
Display: Anton (SIL Open Font License), for titles, name cards, numbers and readouts. Brush accent: Yuji Syuku (OFL) for the 忍 mark, boss titles and era stamps. Both self-hosted as subsets under 120 KB in Phase 3.

    
Effects language per nature
Fire: flame tongues and embers that rise. Wind: crescent blades and leaves that slice outward. Lightning: a flash, branching bolts, fast sparks. Earth: rock chunks under gravity and low dust. Water: sprays under gravity, a ground splash. Taijutsu: speed lines and a white impact star. Every technique gets its nature's default; the signatures (Rasengan, Chidori, Water Dragon…) are named overrides built from the same parts.

Motion rules
    
      
  • Idle breathe 1.3 px over 2.6 s; walk bob with a leg swing; attack: 80 ms anticipation lean, 120 ms lunge, 160 ms recover.
    • 
      
  • Hit: 60 ms white flash, 6–10 px knockback, shake 6; Ultimates and specials: hit-stop 70 ms, shake 9–12; the clash: 0.35× slow motion for about 0.5 s, shake 14 on resolution.
    • 
      
  • Cast: the hand comes forward for the wind-up; KO: the figure falls (80°) and fades over 0.5 s; clones and summons dissolve in smoke.
    • 
      
  • Camera: ±8 px idle drift; boss intro push-in to 1.07× over 1.2 s; the clash pushes to 1.08×.
    • 
      
  • UI: screens slide 6 px and fade over 0.2 s; the cut-in wipes in over 130 ms, holds 0.75 s, wipes out; readouts pop from 1.6× to 1× in 160 ms.
    • 
      
  • Low VFX or reduced motion: no particles, shake, slow motion or push-in; flashes become 40 ms fades.
